cc-calsync

cc-calsync is a small personal tool that keeps one person's three Google Calendars from double-booking. When an event appears on one of them, it adds a busy block at the same time on the other two.

A block carries only a generic label such as "Busy". Nothing from the original event crosses over: not its title, description, place or guests. Only the owner uses the tool; it is not offered to anyone else.
